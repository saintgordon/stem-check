      003 / SAINT GORDON COLLABORATE PROTOCOL

      How to Prepare & Send Your Stems

      Stem Check automates the technical side of Saint Gordon’s free vocal mixing & feature transmission protocol.

      Open Collaborate ↗
      01

      Completely Unedited Takes

      Export vocal files as recorded. Leave natural breaths intact — avoid awkward hard cuts or clicks at 0:00.

      02

      100% Dry — No Effects Added

      Bypass all reverb, delay, chorus, widener, EQ, and autotune plugins prior to bounce so vocals can be sculpted cleanly.

      03

      Export All Tracks from 0:00

      Every stem must start at Bar 1 (0:00) at one matching sample rate (44.1 kHz or 48 kHz, 24-bit WAV) so takes lock straight to the beat.

      04

      Label, Zip & Send References

      Name tracks clearly (Main Vox, Main Double, Adlibs, Harmonies, Beat), zip your folder, and include 1–2 reference songs.
